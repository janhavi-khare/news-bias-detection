# News Bias Detection

An AI/ML-based system for detecting and analyzing political bias in news articles.

## Problem Statement

News articles can present information with political bias through word choice,
framing, source selection, and sentiment. This project aims to automatically
identify the political orientation of news content and estimate its degree of bias.

## Objectives

- Detect political bias in news articles
- Classify articles into left, center, or right political orientation
- Analyze linguistic patterns associated with political bias
- Provide model confidence and explainability
- Compare different machine learning and NLP approaches
